# @frankx-ai/agentic-health-os

Private personal-health operating system for Agentic Life OS.

Agentic Health OS organizes nutrition, training, sleep, recovery, stress, medical-record prep, clinician handoff, and Health Intelligence System protocol packets. It is the personal product layer, not the biomedical research gateway.

## Safety stance

- No diagnosis or reassurance.
- No lab, pathology, imaging, biomarker, or genetic interpretation.
- No medication, supplement, dose, treatment, or delay recommendations.
- No replacement for licensed clinicians, emergency care, or care-team instructions.

Biomedical database lookup belongs in `@frankx-ai/life-sciences-researcher-is`.
